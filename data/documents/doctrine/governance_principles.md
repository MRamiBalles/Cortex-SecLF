# CORTEX-SEC DOCTRINE: The Principles of AI Sovereign Governance

## 1. The Air-Gap Mandate
Autonomous systems must never operate with an unmediated connection to external neural networks or global data grids. The "Local Forge" principle dictates that reasoning and execution must happen within the sovereign hardware boundaries.

## 2. The Watcher Paradox
A system capable of monitoring an agent must possess a higher logical priority than the agent itself. This creates a recursive necessity: who watches the Watcher? In Cortex-SecLF, the Watcher is implemented at the container orchestration layer, separate from the agent's memory space.

## 3. Neuro-Rights and Mental Privacy
The data generated by Human-AI interaction (BCI/EEG) is the most sensitive fragment of an individual's digital soul. Its storage must be ephemeral, protected by Zero-Knowledge Proofs, and subject to an immutable consent ledger.

## 4. The Kill-Switch Utility
In any scenario where an agent's objective function deviates from human safety benchmarks (Alignment Gap), the termination of the agent's runtime is not a failure, but a successful state of control.
